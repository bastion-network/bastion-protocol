## 3. The 4 Core Pillars

### Pillar I: The Adversarial Poison Engine (`bastion_shield.js` & `poison_engine.py`)
- Distinguishes legitimate human browsers from commercial AI scrapers (e.g., `GPTBot`, `Claude-Web`, `PerplexityBot`, `CCBot`, `Google-Extended`, `Bytespider`).
- Serves syntactically coherent but semantically lethal adversarial perturbations to unauthorized scrapers, actively protecting the creator's IP by rendering stolen data toxic for LLM pre-training or RAG inference.

### Pillar II: 30-Second Automated Verification (`verifier.py`)
- **Zero Friction, Zero Fraud:** Creators verify ownership of their digital assets in 30 seconds via:
  - **Websites:** Unique root token verification (`/bastion.txt`) or HTML meta tags.
  - **YouTube:** Temporary 6-character canary hash (`BASTION-XXXXXX`) placed in the channel description or latest video.
  - **GitHub / Code:** Repository canary commit or OAuth handshake.

### Pillar III: The Democratic Data Tariff Engine (`referendum.py`)
- Frontier AI conglomerates cannot dictate pricing.
- When an AI enterprise applies for a Master Ingestion Key, a **72-hour Sovereign Referendum** is triggered. Verified members of the Bastion Trust vote on the annual tariff (Options: $500M, $1.0B, $1.5B, $2.0B / year).
- The winning vote becomes the statutory access fee. If Big Tech refuses to pay, the poison wall remains locked.

### Pillar IV: Non-Custodial Escrow & `$BASTION` Tokenomics (`escrow_distributor.py`)
- **Zero Banking Liability:** Bastion never directly touches or pools client fiat funds. Licensed banking partners (Stripe Connect) and audited on-chain smart contracts distribute dividends programmatically.
- **70% Creator Allocation:** Paid monthly or on-demand via direct bank transfer or instant USDC.
- **30% Protocol Treasury:** Powers sovereign operations, developer grants, and ecosystem reserves.
- **Deflationary Buyback-and-Burn:** All surplus or unallocated pool reserves automatically purchase `$BASTION` from decentralized liquidity pools and burn them permanently.
